Fees Structure

This topic gives entire information of financial transactions that shall happen in the organization. You need to know what all kind of fee is collected like regular fee, admission fee, annual day fee, balance fee, etc., Besides this, every grade has various fees slab. All these have been clearly described in this topic along with related charts.

Fees Type

Following kinds of Financial transactions happens in every branch through cash / card / online transfer / Paytm etc.,

Sl Fee Type Description
1 Regular fees Fees that will be collected for present month.
2 Advance fees Students can pay fees in advance for 3 months / 6 months / 12 months and can avail discount.
3 Balance fees If any student has not paid previous month / months fee then it is called balance fee, which has to be collected along with Regular fees.
4 Extra class fees In general all students get 8 classes per month. If students take extra class then it has to be charged on pro-rata basis.
5 Class-use Instrument fees If students utilize the instruments provided by organization for class purpose then a nominal fee of Rs. 200/- is charged per month.
6 Course materials fees Different courses demand different course materials like Practical book, Song book, Tab book, etc., Related payments should be collected.
7 Exam fees Students appearing for different board exams would pay Exam fees.
8 Annual day training fees This is applicable for Students who are given exclusive training to perform in Annual day shows.
9 Cultural Tours Tour to famous temples all over India are arranged frequently and students are given an opportunity to perform. Cultural tour fees include Travelling charges (To and Fro), Boarding charges, Refreshment Charges, etc.,
10 Others Include Instrument Renting charges, Instrument Repair charges, Choreography charges, Costumes charges, etc.,

General Instructions :

  • One time Admission / Registration fees is Rs. 800 /-
  • Admission fees and monthly fees are not refundable / transferable.
  • Monthly fee and admission fee has to be paid in advance.
  • Monthly fees is collected within 10th of every month.
  • If students are taking admission somewhere in the middle of the month i.e., between 12th and 25th, if possible charge full fees then calculate and give extra classes.
  • If a student is taking admission between 25th and 30th, ask them to start the class from 1st of next month and just collect the admission fees.
  • 30% of the fees shall be collected despite complete absence of the student.
