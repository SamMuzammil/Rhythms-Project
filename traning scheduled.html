Training Schedule

The training schedule is very well set with clarity on

  • Topics that your designation needs to cover
  • Time allotted
  • Day schedule

There will be many topics / subtopics, some of these might confuse you, or require realistic demonstrations / explanation. Jot down all those, and get them clarified by the HR manager who will be visiting you at the end of the day for about two hours.

And here, your training session begins. Enjoy it and learn. Be interested to know every topic in detail as if you have enrolled for the classes. Be aware that any information you pass on will result in great motivation and the learner will make the right choice which can lead to the flow of good artists in this world.
