Annual Plan

Rhythms is well-known for conducting a wide variety of events, exams and plenty of activities. As all these need to be covered over a period of 12 months, an annual calendar has been planned that provides a clear picture of what activity happens in every month. At the same time this calendar becomes a guide for new joinee to know the activities ahead / in the forthcoming months.

Annual Plan

  1. Orientation class
  2. Hifliers
  3. Master Session
  4. Workshop
  5. High achievers concert
  6. Graduation ceremony
  7. "Prathibhankura" — Rising Stars Talent Shows

    a. Course-specific events

  8. Teachers performance
  9. "Flames" - Annual Fest

    a. Annual Day - Once in a year (For all branches)

    b. Grand Finale - Once in a year

  10. State Level Inter School - College Talent Competition
  11. Album Production

    a. Cover version

    b. Own composition

  12. Lecture Demonstrations (Course-wise) - Twice in a year (July & March)
  13. Examinations of All Boards

    a. Rhythms Exams

    b. University Exams

    c. Associated exam boards

  14. Music & Dance Festivals

    a. Sri Purandara Dasa & Sri Thyagaraja Aradhana Mahotsava.

    b. Gana Yogi Pandit Puttaraja Gawai — "Naada Namana."

    c. Guru Poornima.

    d. Saraswathi Pooja.

    e. Shri K. S. Hazari Prasad Memorial Programme - "Smaranaanjali."

    f. Music and Dance Programmes at Idagunji, Sringeri, Udupi, Kolluru, TTD.

  15. General Programmes

    a. Legendary concerts

    b. Budding artist concert

    c. Devotional project

    d. ICCR

    e. Friday bash

    f. Sankasti

    g. Sadakas program
