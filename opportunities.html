Students band

This initiative brings discipline, co-ordination, multi-tasking, teamwork and creativity.
Being in a band gives students a sense of pride and accomplishment.

High Flyer

Serious and good learners in all grades in all courses are provided with an opportunity
to get selected as High Flyers. High flyers enjoy following benefits:

  • Special training sessions from Chairman and guest artists
  • Discount on Projects of Rhythms like album, shows, events, etc.,
  • Opportunity to be a part of Rhythms students band
  • Fee reduction for higher grade learning based on certain parameters.

    • Scholarship

    To make fine arts reachable to underprivileged and visually impaired, the college

    We are closely associated with Industry stalwarts, connoisseurs across the world & with National

    Youtube Elite

    students. Best performances of these are selected and specially trained to appear in the
