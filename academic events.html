Academic Events

Events are the evidence of what a student has learnt. These carve as milestone for students to become artist. Annually the organization conducts an array of two kind of events - Academic events and Cultural events. In this topic, academic related events are explained in detail.

Chairman Sessions

Chairman Dr. Vishwanath Prasad regularly conduct workshops, special practical & theory
sessions to share his vast knowledge that he has gained over the years. These sessions
have witnessed tremendous transformation in students with respect to the way they
look at their subject, embracing the learning process more happily.

Lecture Demonstrations

Free lecture demonstrations and seminars are held frequently in the organization and at schools,
colleges & corporate premises educating masses to understand, appreciate, enjoy and follow
pure art forms.

High achiever's concert

Toppers and distinction holders are allowed to present their favourite piece
in High Achievers concert.This becomes a great occasion to share their achievement
with a wider audience in a supportive & celebrating setting.

Sadhakas Programs

Includes around 10-15 days of exhaustive performance-oriented sessions by renowned
artists covering different genres, exclusive theory, audio - visuals, listening sessions
and all-round techniques required for high-level performance and musicianship
skills. This is a wonderful opportunity for budding talents to understand industry
needs and get closer to their journey of becoming an artist.
