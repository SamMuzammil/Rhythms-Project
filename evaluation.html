Evaluation

You are now aware that the organization is quite diverse and different compared to other industries. In order to confirm your knowledge about the organization and its needs, we have formed a few points based on which the evaluation will take place and until a candidate passes the evaluation they will not be able to deliver completely as this is a service industry and all activities are chain-linked.

One misguided counseling can permanently damage the future of a student. Any wrongly guided process or steps can create unwanted situations in many departments. One thing for sure, once a candidate will pass this evaluation, they can represent the organization very strongly, confidently and can handle the students, and deliver the best.
