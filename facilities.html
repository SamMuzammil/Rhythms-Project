Audio - Visuals

Fine arts is versatile & has been followed throughout the world with different school of thoughts. Hence Audio-visual sessions are made part of curriculum wherein legendary and eminent artists biography, lectures, perspectives, illustrations, videos and recordings are screened. These sessions are shorter, focused, crispier and interactive kind helping learners explore different genres and methodologies.

Jam Room

Hi-fi spacious jam room has almost anything a musician might need. Enriched with advanced instruments & gadgets, it suffice the need of both thirsty bands and classical ensembles. Artists advise, Sound engineer & live recording facility are also available. Excellent interiors with planned lighting arrangements support photo & video shoots as well.

Charges :

  • Ranges between Rs.500/- to Rs. 1,300/‐ per hour based on facilities availed.
  • Special discount applicable for frequent visitors.
