Personnel in Charge

You have to be aware that there are many job profiles in the organization. We have created a short, condensed summary of whom you can meet for what reason so that you can carry out your jobs and claim the rightful support.

Monthly Performance report

This particular stream being a service industry, lots of people are working in different profiles. Hence a clear performance report is maintained about the staff to understand who is really delivering the best and who is just backing. This report will be prepared by the HR manager at the end of the month and at the beginning of the next month, a meeting will be conducted discussing pros and cons. Good performers will be recognized and appreciated, and those who are not up to the mark will be advised and given some time to improve or might face termination.

Attendance Book

This book is printed after roll-on on the first day of each month. It contains details of each and every active student, their contact numbers, grades, attendance etc. The front office admin will mark the student attendance and update it later on to the software. This book will also be used to call & update reports of students. There will be a circle marked for those who have paid the fees so that based on which one can call up and remind them about their pending fees. Any management team can get in the class to mark the attendance and will be able to remind the student on spot to pay the Fees.

Note: Click here to view sample "Attendance Book".

Student Status Report (SSR)

This report will be available with the front office admin and will be handed over to the teacher every time they get inside the class. In this particular report, each and every detail about the student, in terms of events, activities, exams, and specially fees details received for the corresponding month will be entered. This gives the clear picture to the teachers about the overall status of a student including his attendance and payment details. This report will also carry the acronym sheet in the first page so that the teacher can plan for alloting students for the events.

Note: Click here to view sample "SSR Report"

Acronym Sheet

This will be the first sheet that will be available in the attendance book as well as the student status report. This acronym sheet carries a description of each and every event, opportunities, exams etc., a student would undergo in his entire journey with rhythms. Acronym details are mentioned next to the name of every student and would be updated time to time. Any new non-teaching staff/teacher can look at the acronym mentioned next to the student name and make out about his overall presence in the organization.

Note: Click here to view sample "Acronym Sheet"

PPT of all Types

Lots of events and activities happen at rhythms. The need is very unique and different for each type. It will be new for joinees to understand and implement the system to be followed. Hence a Wonderful Power Point presentation has been prepared, which carries details of how any event or activity has to be conducted. Checklists, photo samples, video samples, etc. are made available. Hence, whenever there is an event to happen, you can watch the relevant PPT that has been prepared. Then jot down any queries you have and get it cleared by seniors.

Your Journey

For all new joinees a welcome booklet "Your Journey" is provided which eases their learning journey at Rhythms. The document throws light on the career aspects pertaining to fine arts, facilities and opportunities provided by the organization towards reaching the same and what they need to demand from teachers.
