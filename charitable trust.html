Charitable Trust

Rhythms has set up a charitable trust called Hazari Prasad foundation for the benefit of visually impaired and underprivileged. The trust imparts free fine arts education to needy. We wish you to pass this info to as many people, As you never know if someone comes forward to support with one good piece of information being passed by you. The details of trust and its activities are exlusively included in this topic.

Rhythms has set-up a Charitable Trust-"Hazari Prasad Foundation" in memory of late Sri K.S. Hazari Prasad, father of Dr. Vishwanath Prasad H. This Trust was inaugurated by The Governor of Karnataka Sri T. N. Chaturvedi and Bharat Ratna Pandit Bhimsen Joshi in 2004. Foundation's objective is to keep alive Sri K.S. Hazari Prasad's memory and his services to the society.

The projects undertaken are:

  • Supporting visually impaired & the underprivileged by giving free Fine arts education,Instruments and Medical aid.
  • Scholarship for pursuing fine arts to deserving and underprivileged budding artists.
  • Providing platform & Medical aid to the underprivileged Artists.
  • Recognizing and Awarding with "Kala Seva Puraskar" which carries a citation, cash reward, etc. to Artists who have contributed immensely in the field of fine arts but have relatively not been in the limelight.

You can be a part of this noble cause by:

  • Sponsoring Fine arts education / Music instruments / Scholarship to the visually impaired & underprivileged.
  • Donating in cash / kind / used musical instruments, etc.
  • Providing financial assistance to the underprivileged & needy artists for their medical cause.
  • Celebrating any memorable occasions of your family at any of the blind schools / orphanage / charity homes shortlisted by us.

This noble cause requires generous support.
We are sure that every individual would like to participate in this initiative.

Donations to Hazari Prasad Foundation are exempt U/S 80G of income tax act , 1961
