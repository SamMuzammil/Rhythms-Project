Programs available, Associated Exam boards & Courses offered

The primary function of the organization is to impart fine arts education. Hence different programs have been structured and made available. Some programs may take long time, based on the course demand and few are shorter programs and some are designed for speedy stage performance. This topic gives relevant details of the same.

Diploma programs: We offer Undergraduate, Graduate & Postgraduate diplomas for the following Vocal, Instrumental, Dance and Visual arts sections as applicable. Structured curriculum with gradation system on par with International standards is formulated.

Learners have the advantage of pursuing diploma programs in Part Time (2 days a week, 1 hr duration per day), Full Time (5 days a week, 5 hrs duration per day) or Online.

Short-term Courses

We offer wide range of short-term courses periodically in all sections pertaining to "Vocal, Instrumental, Dance and Visual arts". Generally, these are designed for three months duration carrying basic and important elements necessary for foundation level performances, catering to enthusiasts who do not get opportunity to learn fine arts due to time constraint.

Intensive Programs

Includes around 30 - 40 master sessions by eminent artists covering all-round needs for the finest learning and performing aspects. Exclusive curriculum to achieve professional and personal goals is set.

Online Certification

Through this unique and distinct program, learners from different part of the world can appear for our exams and certification.

Exam Boards

Rhythms Exam Board : We follow structured curriculum for every course with "gradation system" on par with International standards integrated with innovative teaching methodologies. This enables students to appear for multiple exam boards especially for all "Indian Classical courses".

  • There are totally 13 Grades, Some courses are up to 6 grades, some are up to 9 grades and some are up to 13 grades.
  • In Rhythms board the "Full time" learners need to cover all the chapters described in syllabus and books, when compared to "Part time" learners who need to cover a minimum of 70 percent of the same.
  • "Direct Entry" for Rhythms Exam Board is based on "Age criteria and qualifying certificate" pursued from other exam boards. Students can directly appear for higher grade exams by clearing the qualifying test.
  • Based on the course and instruments demand preferred age is set by our experience. However for students below the preferred age a separate grade termed "Initial" is made available.

Associated Exam Boards : We are associated with National and International Exam Boards. The structured syllabus, standard teaching methodologies and exam pattern setup at Rhythms enables learners to appear for multiple exam boards.

Courses Offered (Online)

General Instructions for taking Online classes:

  • Adjust your camera if it is too low or high. Don't get too close. Your camera should be at eye level.
  • Position yourself so that most of the light is coming from in front of you (behind your monitor / mobile), instead of behind you.
  • Look at the camera when you're talking. This tactic will mimic the in-person feeling of eye contact.
  • It's important to gauge reactions by looking at the screen, but alternating that with looking at the camera makes the opposite person feel like you're really talking to them.
  • Your surroundings say a lot about you. Let's make sure that they say the right things. Have a simple background.
  • If you have a window behind you, shut the blinds. Otherwise, you will be backlit.
  • Find a quiet well lit location. Ensure there is no background noise - fans, washing machines, loud music, etc. Ensure there is adequate lighting if you are using the webcam video.
  • Even though it's tempting, try not to multitask too much. And if you're going to, at least mute yourself.
  • When possible, use a good quality camera and headset instead of your computer's built-in ones.
  • If you are taking classes on mobile then the hold mobile in landscape mode and use Mobile stands
