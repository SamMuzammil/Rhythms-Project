About Rhythms

This organization is very unique not only in Karnataka but in entire India. Generally, nobody has ventured so deep about this particular industry as Rhythms has been. We are working in multiple streams, so there is a strong need for you to understand what is this organization all about, how it came to existence, what's our mission, it's functionality and presently where we stand.

Introduction

Welcome to the world of Fine arts - A universal language
The divine gift that never considers age, gender, religion or the monetary aspect

Rhythms - a landmark premier Institution was established with a motto of creating awareness and promotion of fine arts in the society especially amongst the youth, to understand, appreciate and follow pure art forms.

The very name "Rhythms" conjures the musical movement with time. Established in 1996, Rhythms has created revolutionary highs in the sphere of fine arts, educating the masses by organizing concerts, lecture demonstrations, competitions, charity shows and social activities along with remarkable professional training.

We offer part time, full time and online courses related to 28 disciplines in Vocal, Instrumental, Dance and Visual arts in Indian and Western styles, providing "University Certified" programs with a credit system. Presently the organization cater more than 4,500 plus active students by around 125 esteemed faculty at our 7 branches prominently located across Bangalore for over two decades.

The devoted and disciplined voyage has made us the topmost college for Fine arts education not only in Karnataka but in entire India. Rhythms is closely associated with Industry stalwarts, connoisseurs across the world and with National - International exam boards making its presence felt globally.

Journey

Through sheer perseverance to spread the knowledge of fine arts Dr. Vishwanath Prasad H. started teaching music to a handful of aspirant guitar students. Later in 1996 the first branch of Rhythms was established by him at Kumarapark West and was dedicated to his father late Sri K. S. Hazari Prasad - an ardent music lover. In a short span, he instituted six more branches exclusively dedicated to following gems of Indian classical music:

It is a matter of pride and honour that these exponents have had inaugurated our branches and as well performed for Rhythms. As a mark of respect to their eternal contribution, a paving stone etched with their name has been set-up at the respective branch. Presently the college stands tall in Bangalore and has become India's largest college delivering structured fine arts education where "Tradition Meets Modernity"

Why Rhythms

Recognized and recommended by the Fine arts Fraternity as we are:

  1. Following structured curriculum with gradation system on par with International standards, integrated with innovative teaching methodologies enabling students to learn and collaborate with different genres as well to appear for multiple exam boards.
  2. Affiliated & Centre to prestigious Exam boards such as Trinity College London, Gandharva Mahavidyalaya, Karnataka Secondary Board, Bangiya Sangeet Parishad, NSTSE ...
  3. Pioneer in providing platforms for learners through talent shows, competitions, seminars, lecture demonstrations, workshops, annual fest, music concerts ...
  4. Enriched with state-of-art facilities such as sophisticated instruments & gadgets, studio, jam room, auditorium ...
  5. Successful in training students and emerging artists to win in National and International competitions, shows and youth exchange programmes.
  6. Providing path carving opportunities for aspirants through album releases.
  7. Instrumental in producing quality artists in the industry which is the need of the hour.
  8. Immensely contributing in educating the masses to understand, appreciate and follow pure art forms through diversified programmes.
