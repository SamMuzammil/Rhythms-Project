Counseling

This is the most important part of the admission. Now after studying this training manual you might get puzzled to make a choice in learning fine arts. Imagine the situation of a learner who wishes to enroll. Hence careful counselling is very much needed.

Important things to be understood before counselling :

Sl Criteria Description
1 Understand the requirement of enquiry Find out what exactly learner wants to be (Vocalist / lead Instrumentalist / rhythm Instrumentalist / Dancer / Painter). Then try to find whether its for hobby / career purpose.
2 Age Age becomes a prime criteria during course selection. For instance if a child below 6 years chooses to learn guitar, then it is highly impossible as the child may not be able to hold the guitar. Similarly if the child wishes to go for Keyboard then finger strength matters a lot. In such case suggest Drawing - painting / Western dance as these strictly do not demand physical strength. Similarly a 6 year child might not be able to reach bass pedal on a drum kit. So counsel carefully and suggest according to their age group.
3 Gender Some times Gender too plays a important role in selecting course. Often boys have more tendancy to opt Guitar and drums course than girls, Similarly girls choose to learn Keyboard / violin / Bharathanatyam but hardly we see any boy opting Bharathanatyam. Hence counsel wisely.
4 Familiar language Only for vocal learning, language plays a barrier. If a north indian wish to pick carnatic vocal it would be a wrong choice, reason he wouldn't be familiar with south indian languages which will be used in carnatic music and vice-versa.
5 Interest Your prime focus should be on to find out what learner is interested whether vocal / Instruments / Dance / arts and then give the choice of styles applicable classical / western.
6 Reason for Joining the course There will be different type of learners. Some may want to pursue course as a passion / hobby and while some wants to create their career in fine arts. While some learners who are working in corporates would like to learn fine arts as a stress buster. Hence find the reason for him / her to join course as this will bond interest in the organization for longer period of time.
7 Modernity Though we teach both classical and western styles, try to find what is the learner's choice. People have a feel that learning western music is modern and classical music are traditional but it is not so.
8 Financial condition In some courses there is a need of money apart from monthly fees to buy instruments. This might give burden to some. Hence advice courses which do not need instruments.
